Terms & Privacy

Privacy Policy
100% Local On-Device Storage

Monolith Vault operates entirely on your device. Your notes, photos, videos, and password hashes are stored exclusively in local device storage (IndexedDB and LocalStorage). No files, metadata, analytics, or credentials are ever transmitted to external servers.

Passwords are never stored in plain text. They are converted locally using SHA-256 cryptographic hashing prior to storage.

Security Mechanics
Lockout, Decoy & Wipe

Failed Attempt Lockout: Entering an incorrect password 5 times consecutively on the unlock screen activates a mandatory 1-hour security lockout with all touch and keyboard inputs disabled.

Decoy (Recovery) Mode: Unlocking with your decoy password opens a separate view showing only the specific notes and media items you explicitly marked for decoy visibility.

All-Reset Password: Entering your reset password on the unlock screen immediately and permanently deletes all stored notes, photos, videos, and vault configuration on this device.

Password Changes: Modifying any password in Settings requires your current password and allows a maximum of 3 attempts before locking the session.

Terms of Use
User Responsibility

Because Monolith Vault uses zero cloud storage and zero remote accounts, forgotten passwords cannot be recovered by any third party. You are solely responsible for remembering your Primary, Decoy, and Reset passwords and maintaining personal backups of critical media.

Clearing your browser or app data on Android will also erase locally stored vault files. Use the Reset Password with caution, as formatted data cannot be restored.
